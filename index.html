Ergomotion: REBA Assessment

Photo and video posture assessment for iPhone and iPad.

Support and feedback

Email sufril@usm.my for support, questions or feedback. Include your app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include identifiable photos, videos or assessment records unless you have permission to share them.

Getting started

  1. Allow camera access and position one person with their full body visible in good lighting.
  2. Choose Photo or Video, then capture a photo or start recording. Existing images and videos can also be imported.
  3. Review REBA results and enter the appropriate load, coupling, activity and wrist inputs.
  4. Open History to review saved assessments, replay video, export assessment data or create a PDF report.

Common questions

Why does a result show Unknown?

Required body landmarks may be obscured or insufficiently visible. Reposition the camera, improve lighting and keep the whole body visible. In preview, capture a photo or start recording to begin assessment.

Why is AI reporting unavailable?

Optional AI descriptions require compatible Apple Intelligence hardware, supported system settings and downloaded on-device models. Local base PDF reports remain available without AI.

Where is the LiDAR switch?

It appears only on supported devices. LiDAR records torso surface depth and does not change REBA scoring.

How do I delete an assessment?

Delete it from History. Copies exported, shared or saved to Photos must be deleted separately.

Assessment limitations

Ergomotion is still being tested. Measurement and scoring accuracy have not been established. Results support preliminary ergonomic screening and require review by a competent assessor. The app does not diagnose medical conditions, certify workplace safety or replace professional assessment.

Privacy

Assessment processing and optional AI report generation take place on your device. Read the Privacy Policy.
