Ergomotion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Ergomotion: REBA Assessment is created by Associate Professor Dr. Ahmad Sufril Azlan Mohamed. This policy describes the current version of the app.

Data collection

The developer does not automatically collect or receive your assessment data. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Camera, photos and videos

Camera access is used to capture photos and videos for posture assessment. You may also select photos or videos to import. Processing takes place on your device. The app does not record audio. Photos access is requested when needed to save assessment output to your Photos library.

Assessment data and reports

Saved assessments may contain images, videos, body landmark coordinates, estimated joint angles, REBA results and capture settings. Optional assessor names, email addresses, phone numbers, task descriptions and notes are stored locally and may appear in exported reports.

Optional AI and LiDAR features

On compatible devices, optional AI report generation uses Apple's on-device models. Assessment content is not sent to an external AI service by this app.

On supported devices, optional LiDAR processing measures torso surface depth. Depth measurements and status information may be saved with the assessment. Full depth maps are not saved. LiDAR does not change REBA scoring.

Storage, export and deletion

Assessments and reports are stored on your device. You control exports and sharing through the system sharing tools. Recipients and services you choose may process shared files under their own privacy policies.

You can delete saved assessments from the app's history. Copies previously exported, shared or saved to Photos must be deleted separately. Depending on your device settings, app data and Photos may be included in Apple backups or iCloud services.

Feedback

If you choose to email feedback, the developer receives the information and attachments you send, including your email address. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and investigate reported issues. Please avoid sending identifiable assessment material unless you have permission to share it.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's privacy practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
